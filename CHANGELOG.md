# What's new in Gully Cricket

Every release is listed here, newest first. To update, download the new APK from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and open it on your phone. It installs over the old app and keeps your teams, players and matches.

---

## [1.2.2] - 2026-10-19 · A proper trophy for the day poster

### Day poster
- **Player of the day now gets a real MVP trophy:** a gold cricket cup with crossed bats, a ball and a star, standing on a black base with an "MVP" plate. A soft gold glow sits behind it, and the star rises just above the panel.
- The small medals next to the orange and purple caps are gone, so each cap stands on its own.

Nothing else changes. Your teams, players and matches stay as they are.

## [1.2.1] - 2026-10-19 · Liquid glass motion

This release is about feel: the app moves like glass and liquid. Layout, colours and icons are unchanged.

### Bottom tab bar
- **The highlight moves like liquid.** Tap Home, Leaderboard or Matches and the lime highlight stretches as it slides, then settles with a small wobble.
- **Drag the highlight.** Put your finger on it and slide. It turns into a clear glass lens that follows your finger, and lets go on the nearest tab.
- **Swipe between tabs.** Swipe left or right anywhere on Home, Leaderboard or Matches to move to the next tab.

### Scrub any choice
- **Press and slide across any row of choices** to pick one: overs, balls per over, retire-at, max overs, house rules, Heads or Tails, which team called, Bat or Bowl, team colours, opening batters and bowler, scorecard tabs, run-out options, and language, theme and look in Settings.
- A clear glass drop follows your finger, you can still read the option under it, and the phone gives a light tick at each option. Let go to choose.
- A normal tap works exactly as before, and moving your finger up or down still scrolls the page.

### Switches and options
- **Switches can be dragged.** Slide the knob across and let go past halfway to flip it.
- **Liquid switch.** While you press a switch, the knob becomes a glass lens over the track. When it flips, it stretches like a drop of liquid on the way across.
- **Two- and three-way options** (overs, balls per over, language, theme, look) stretch their highlight as it glides to the new choice.

### Light on glass (Glass look only)
- **Touch glint.** A soft light blooms where your finger presses any button, key, chip, list row or tab.
- **Moving glare.** A faint band of light slides across glass panels, list rows, the tab bar and the button bar as you scroll. Sheets get a softly lit top edge.
- The **Solid** look (Settings → Look) stays plain, for bright sun and older phones.

### Small touches
- Things that appear mid-screen (toss steps, newly added players, the common player's name field, error messages) rise in gently instead of popping.
- Today's numbers on Home count up when the screen opens.
- Messages at the top slide down and fade out the way they came in.

All motion is quick (about 0.3 seconds) and never delays a tap. If your phone is set to reduce motion, the app uses calm fades instead.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-18 · Dashboard, awards and the gold coin

A big update. It also includes everything from the 1.1 preview, which was never released on its own.

### Players and teams
- **Regular players.** On first launch the app asks "Who plays usually?". Add the names once. If you're updating, your saved teams become this list automatically.
- **Pick from regulars.** Each team has a tick list of your regulars. Anyone already in the other team is greyed out. "+ Add" puts a new name in the team and in the list.
- **Randomise teams.** Tick who's playing and tap Make teams to get two even sides. With an odd number, one random player plays for both sides; tap Change to choose who, or Add one more. Shuffle again re-rolls.
- Teams start as **Team 1** and **Team 2**, and you can rename them as before.
- Names are capitalised automatically ("chethan" becomes "Chethan").
- The saved-team chips and the example teams are gone.

### Scoring
- **Wides and no-balls are always bowled again.** The two re-bowl switches are gone. Old matches keep the rules they were played with.
- **Undo says what it will undo**, for example "Undo · 4", "Undo · Ravi bowling" or "Undo · openers".
- **Change the bowler before the over's first ball** by tapping the bowler's name.
- **Fours and sixes pop on the over tracker:** an orange ripple for a four, a purple burst with sparks for a six.
- The score rolls up on each ball. A wicket shakes the scoreboard red and the wicket ball drops in. A new over's balls sweep in.

### Home, Leaderboard and Matches
- **Bottom tabs** for Home, Leaderboard and Matches.
- **Home is a dashboard:** New match, the live match, Play again (toss or winner picks), today's matches, runs, fours and sixes, the day's awards, and the last result.
- **Matches tab:** every match with its result, scorecard and poster. It's read-only, so there are no toss or rematch buttons there.
- **Today's leaderboard:**
  - **Player of the day:** whoever won performer of the match most often today. A tie goes to whoever has more total points.
  - **Orange cap:** the top 5 run scorers.
  - **Purple cap:** the top 5 wicket takers.
  - Players are matched by name, so someone who switches teams still adds up.
- **Day poster** with the player of the day, the orange cap and the purple cap, ready to share on WhatsApp.

### Toss
- **3D gold coin.** Polished metal with moving reflections, raised bat-and-ball (heads) and stumps (tails), and a ridged edge. It tosses, spins, lands with a bounce and buzzes. Very low-memory phones get the previous coin.

### Motion
- Screens slide in from the side they come from. Sheets slide up and can be swiped down by their handle.
- Switches slide, option highlights glide, ticks grow in and colours fade smoothly. The screen now updates in place, so typing and scroll position are never disturbed.
- The match result gets a short burst of confetti.

### Under the hood
- The 3D coin uses three.js r128 (MIT licence), bundled so it works offline.
- The app grew from about 2.8 MB to 3.0 MB.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-11 · First release

- **Score ball by ball** with big run keys, wide, no-ball, bye and leg-bye buttons, and undo for anything.
- **Your gully rules:** overs, balls per over, last batter bats alone, single batter, six is out, free hit, retire at N runs, bowler over limits, plus house rules like one tip one hand.
- **Toss:** call Heads or Tails, say which team called, flip the in-app coin or enter a real coin, and the winner picks bat or bowl.
- **Wickets the gully way:** bowled, caught, stumped or run out, then pick the next batter. No fielder names.
- **Ties:** super over, share the match, or a bowl-out.
- **After the match:** continue with the same teams (toss, or the winner picks), edit teams, change rules or start fresh.
- **Performer of the match** from batting and bowling points, and a full scorecard.
- **Result poster** in a bold sports style to share or save.
- **English and ಕನ್ನಡ**, light and dark themes, and a Solid look for bright sun.
- Works fully offline, with no internet permission, ads or accounts. Back up or move matches from Settings → Backup.
